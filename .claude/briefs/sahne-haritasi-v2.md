## The new arc in one paragraph

The glass split stays the home base, but it is no longer the whole film. The story now leaves home three times and
always comes back: into the screen (the AI's own cream world, where she is "upgraded" again and again but walks the same
path and trips on the same mistake), out into the city (by day he sees her in every shop window; by night, on the bus,
she sits in the window reflection and comforts him), and through time (days pile up as cups, notes and a sun/moon disc
that crosses the window). The loudest instrumental moments are carried by whole-frame floods of one colour instead of a
static crack. The last shot is the first shot (the screen, the cursor): the loop closes.

Identity (unchanged): "The human accumulates; the screen resets every morning." New sub-rule from Burak:
**"model değişiyor ama akış değişmiyor"** — every time we see the AI change, it ends in the same pose on the same path.
