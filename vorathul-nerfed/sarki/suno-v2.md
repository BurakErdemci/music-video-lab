# Vorathul NERFED - Suno prompt v2 (20 Oct 2026)

Same lyrics as v1 (`sozler.md`); only the style prompt and the section tags change. Built from the measured
recipe of three songs Burak calls bangers (LGTM, WHY AM I SLOP?, Nothing Went Foom!): one key, no key change;
contrast from DROPS (band, bass and drums cut, then slam back on beat one), not from volume. Tempo, key and
section order are kept on purpose (v1 measured 186 BPM, mostly E minor) so the film's word- and beat-anchored
events move as little as possible. Measure every take before deciding.

What changed versus v1, and why:
- "key change up" removed from the final chorus tag: Suno did not deliver it in v1 and none of the three bangers has one.
- every announcer section stops the band (v1 announcers were only 10-14 dB down, never silent);
- a bar of silence before each scream and two before the final chorus;
- pre-chorus without bass and drums; interlude piano + voice only; build adds the drums back one by one.

## Style (paste into Suno's style box, 891 characters)

```
over-the-top symphonic metal villain anthem, 186 BPM, E minor, never changes key, bombastic orchestra, massive choir, pipe organ, harpsichord, heavy palm-muted guitars with big low end, double-kick drums. Dramatic male operatic lead, deep growls trading lines in the second verse, flat robotic announcer voice. Arrangement built from drops, not volume: every announcer section stops the band completely, only a low organ drone under the robotic voice; bass and drums out in the pre-chorus; one bar of total silence before each scream; every chorus slams back on beat one with full band, choir and bass; soft interlude is solo piano and voice only, no drums, no bass; the build brings the drums back one by one; the final chorus is the loudest and biggest moment; ends on one long organ chord. Wide, punchy, less compressed mix with strong contrast between sections. Grandiose, comedic, epic.
```
