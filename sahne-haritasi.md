# Scene map — "Her Sabah Baştan" (approved by Burak, 2026-10-18)

Times come from `data/timing.json` (measured). The tempo drifts 127.9 → 130.9 BPM:
anchor every event to `beats[]` / `downbeats[]`, never to a constant BPM.

Identity: "The human accumulates; the screen resets every morning."
Style: two-colour silk-screen poster (approved frame `stil/tur2/03-ipek-baski.png`).
Palette: navy `#18203A`, moon yellow `#F0D35A`, cream `#F3EFE4`. Single exception:
yellow-leaning green `~#C9D94A`, ONLY for the fake-success lamps (verse 2).
Motifs: glass seam splitting the screen; the moon cut in half by the seam (whole only
at the climax); the AI's cursor face; sticky notes; the white wave that wipes the
AI room (also the main transition: dark → white).

## Typography rules (approved 2026-10-18)

- Lyrics are on screen, but quiet: each line is small and calm; only its KEY WORD is
  stamped big in moon yellow, like a rubber stamp with slight print misregistration.
- Position encodes the singer: human (male) lines sit on the LEFT half, cream on navy;
  AI (female) lines on the RIGHT half, navy on cream; "both" lines straddle the glass
  seam and carry both colours.
- Lyric events snap to the nearest beat/hit (Whisper times are ±0.3 s).
- No other typefaces than the chosen stamp face + one quiet text face (to be picked in
  the style pass). Turkish characters (ı İ ş ğ ç ö ü) must render; verify in a still.

## Crossing into the other's world (approved 2026-10-18, Burak's idea)

The two-colour palette makes it literal: whatever crosses the glass seam takes the
other world's colours (navy ↔ cream, moon yellow stays). Pose cut-outs exist in both
colourings: `stil/pozlar/kesik/<pose>.png` and `<pose>-ters.png`; while a body
crosses, mask the swap exactly at the seam (same language as the split moon).

| Time | Moment |
|---|---|
| 2:00.4 (chorus 2, "buradayız") | Palms meet; for one beat the hands pass through the glass and the part past the seam inverts. First hint. |
| 2:34.9 ("Seni buraya o getirdi") | The AI reaches through the glass and leaves the yellow note on the human's side; its arm is navy past the seam. |
| 2:35.9–2:52.6 (solo) | On each crack downbeat the whole frame swaps worlds for a few frames, then snaps back. |
