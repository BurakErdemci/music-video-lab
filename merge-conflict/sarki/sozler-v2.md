# Merge conflict - song v2 (draft, 20 Oct 2026)

Rework of `sozler-v1.md` (2:39) with the measured recipe of the three references, aimed at about 3:30.
- Heavy bed + light voices as in WHY AM I SLOP?: standard tuning, big low end, two bright female voices on top.
  "djent", "blast beats" and "screams" from v1 dropped (measured risk: djent/drop tuning muds the mix, "scream"
  can pull in death-metal growls); "shouted chest voice" and "shrieked high ad-libs" instead.
- Tempo 152 (v1 asked 185): fast enough for anime battle energy, slow enough that the duet lines stay clear.
  One key, no key change; contrast from drops and stops.
- Pink is now ORANGE (the two girls are orange-themed and blue-themed; still never named).
- Added: a one-word post-chorus chant, a fourth verse (rebase / squash / git blame), a guitar solo, a growing count
  in each chorus (one conflict -> forty -> nine hundred) and a final "Accept both!" chant before the Build failed joke.

Chaotic, absurd, funny, anime battle energy.
