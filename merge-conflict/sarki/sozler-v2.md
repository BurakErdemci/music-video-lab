# Merge conflict - song v2 (draft, 20 Oct 2026)

Rework of `sozler-v1.md` (2:39) with the measured recipe of the three references, aimed at about 3:30.
- Heavy bed + light voices as in WHY AM I SLOP?: standard tuning, big low end, two bright female voices on top.
  "djent", "blast beats" and "screams" from v1 dropped (measured risk: djent/drop tuning muds the mix, "scream"
  can pull in death-metal growls); "shouted chest voice" and "shrieked high ad-libs" instead.
- Tempo 152 (v1 asked 185): fast enough for anime battle energy, slow enough that the duet lines stay clear.
  One key, no key change; contrast from drops and stops.
- Pink is now ORANGE (the two girls are orange-themed and blue-themed; still never named).
- Singer cues are bracket tags on their own line ([Sweet high idol voice] / [Raspy low rock voice]); the first take sang an
  inline "Girl A:" prefix aloud and gave both parts the same voice (20 Oct 2026).
- Added: a one-word post-chorus chant, a fourth verse (rebase / squash / git blame), a guitar solo, a growing count
  in each chorus (one conflict -> forty -> nine hundred) and a final "Accept both!" chant before the Build failed joke.

## Style (967 characters)

```
kawaii metal, melodic metalcore, anime rock, 152 BPM, E minor, never changes key, straight 4/4, eighth-note kick, double-kick sixteenths in the breakdown and final chorus, high-gain palm-muted guitars with big low end, deep sub bass, bright synth leads, chiptune blips, symphonic stabs, one melodic guitar solo. Duet of two DIFFERENT female singers with clearly different timbres, call-and-response: one sweet, high, bright J-pop idol voice; one low, raspy, aggressive rock voice with shouted lines and shrieked ad-libs, no growls. They alternate lines in verses, trade single words in chants, stack in tight harmony in choruses. Both vocals dry, bright, upfront above the guitars. Flat robotic voice with the music stopped. Built from drops: bass out in verses, one or two bars of silence before full-band re-entries on beat one, solo piano interlude with voices only, huge final chorus. Wide punchy mix, strong contrast. Chaotic, absurd, funny, anime battle energy.
```
