ART STYLE: genuine low-resolution pixel art, as if drawn at 320x200 and scaled up with nearest-neighbour: every pixel a big visible hard square, limited 32-colour palette, dithering only inside the art, no anti-aliasing, no smooth gradients.

SHOT: A side-view pixel art disco floor with flashing coloured tiles, the two tiny pixel girls in a dance battle under a pixel mirror ball, one doing a kick that knocks the other back.

CONCEPT: a key frame for a heavy-metal disco music video. Two rival girls fight to be the star: a dance battle on a disco floor where each one tries to shove the other out of the spotlight and take the foreground for herself. One girl's signature colour is ORANGE, the other's is BLUE. Invent BOTH characters freshly in this art style's own language (faces, hair, outfits, proportions); do NOT default to generic anime girls. They must be clearly two different personalities.

The image must look like it comes from a completely different medium than anime illustration: commit fully to the art style above, its materials, its proportions and its rendering.

Rules:
- NO halftone dot screens, NO print grain, NO film noise, NO scanlines, NO VHS or broken-TV effects laid over the image. Light spots, glows and particles inside the scene are fine.
- A saturated colour riot: many colours, never muddy or washed out; orange and blue lead.
- Landscape 1536x1024.
- The ONLY text allowed: the three words GIT PUSH FORCE as huge disco-sign letters in the scene, each word lit in a different colour, spelled exactly like that. No other text, numbers, logos or watermarks.
